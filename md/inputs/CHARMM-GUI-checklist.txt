CHARMM-GUI settings for the SLC46A1 engraftment construct set
=============================================================

Input Generator > Membrane Builder > Bilayer Builder > Upload PDB.
Run it 3 times, once per file, from md/systems:

  construct__apo_pH7.5.pdb     26 substitutions
  primaries__apo_pH7.5.pdb     20 substitutions
  wt__apo_pH7.5.pdb            no substitutions

Chains            keep chain A, the only chain in each file. No ligand is
                  present, since this is the substrate-free state.
Disulfides        tick the 1 pair CHARMM-GUI offers: 72 with 306. Measured SG to SG at 2.03 angstrom
                  in all 3 files, so the same pair appears in every build. The 
                  9 cysteines are at 72, 96, 172, 195, 237, 306, 336, 405, 410 and no other pair is close.
Missing residues  none inside the chain. The 432 residues span 26 to 457 with no
                  internal gap, so there is nothing for CHARMM-GUI to model.
Terminal groups   neutral caps, ACE and CT3 or their equivalents. Residue 26 is
                  not the start of the protein and residue 457 is not its end:
                  both are where the deposited model stops, so charged NTER and
                  CTER would put formal charges in the middle of the sequence.
Protonation       defaults at the pH the state carries. The arms are not
                  identical in titratable content: Arg, Asp, Glu, His, Tyr differ across the arms
                  and one humanising substitution installs a histidine, so
                  the wild type carries 6 and the others 7.
Lipid             POPC 70 percent with CHOL 30 percent, 90 per leaflet as the target, entered as a ratio.
                  The ratio resolves to 63 POPC, 27 CHOL per leaflet.
Water             22.5 angstrom thickness.
Ions              0.15 M KCl, neutralising. The arms do not carry the same formal
                  charge, construct +2, primaries +1, wt +1, so the counter-ion counts will differ by 1
                  between the extremes. A build whose charge disagrees with the
                  figure above means the wrong file was uploaded.
Force field       CHARMM36m.
Dynamics          GROMACS.


Step by step
------------

1.  Open charmm-gui.org, log in, and choose Input Generator >
    Membrane Builder > Bilayer Builder. The protein is a transporter in a
    bilayer, so this is the builder, not Solution Builder.
2.  Choose "Upload PDB" and upload the first file from md/systems. The three
    files, in the order they are listed above, are:
      construct__apo_pH7.5.pdb
      primaries__apo_pH7.5.pdb
      wt__apo_pH7.5.pdb
    Do one at a time and keep the three downloads apart. The run is the same
    for each, and only the uploaded file changes.
3.  PDB Info page. Keep chain A and nothing else. Leave the het records
    alone, there are none to keep.
4.  On the same page, the disulfide and protonation panel. Tick the pair 72 with 306. Leave the protonation at its defaults.
    Read off which histidine form it assigns at the 5 titratable substituted
    positions, 217, 320, 404, 406, 430, before going on.
5.  Terminal group patching. Set both ends to neutral caps rather than
    charged NTER and CTER, because residue 26 is not the start of the
    protein and residue 457 is not its end.
6.  Orientation. Choose "Run PPM 2.0" and leave every Positioning Option
    unticked. PPM sets the membrane normal along z itself, so a rotation or a
    translation entered by hand afterwards undoes what it just did. The
    computed energy printed above those options is a CHARMM single point on
    the rebuilt coordinates: a very large van der Waals term there comes from
    the side chains and hydrogens CHARMM-GUI has just added, since in the
    uploaded files the closest non-bonded heavy-atom contact is 2.16 angstrom
    (ARG122 NH2 to PHE238 O), and the only pair nearer than that is the 72 with 306 disulfide at 2.03.
    It falls away during minimisation and equilibration, so read it after
    those rather than here.
7.  Lipid page. POPC 70 percent with CHOL 30 percent, 90 per leaflet as the target, symmetric leaflets.
    Enter the ratio, 70 to 30, and leave the counts to the page. It
    resolves to 63 POPC, 27 CHOL per leaflet. The XY box length the page asks
    for in ratio mode is an initial guess and not a value to leave
    blank, since a blank there selects no lipid and the build stops.
8.  Water and ions. 22.5 angstrom water thickness, 0.15 M KCl, neutralising. Check
    the net charge it reports against the figure above for that arm.
9.  Force field and equilibration input. CHARMM36m, and tick GROMACS as the
    dynamics engine. Leave hydrogen mass repartitioning unticked.
    Temperature: 303.15 K.
10. Download the charmm-gui-*.tgz when the build finishes, then repeat from
    step 2 for the next file. Three builds in all.
11. Unpack each archive. Inside is a gromacs/ folder holding
    step5_input.gro, step5_input.pdb, step5_input.psf, topol.top,
    index.ndx, step7_production.mdp.
    The contents of that folder are what go on Drive, without the
    gromacs/ level around them.

Unpack each charmm-gui-*.tgz and put the contents of its gromacs/ folder
on Drive as

  /content/drive/MyDrive/MDs/SLC46A1_engraftment/construct
  /content/drive/MyDrive/MDs/SLC46A1_engraftment/primaries
  /content/drive/MyDrive/MDs/SLC46A1_engraftment/wt

A build folder is recognised by holding step5_input.pdb, so the folder names
above matter and the names CHARMM-GUI gives the archives do not.

The 5 titratable positions among the 26 substituted ones are 217, 320, 404, 406, 430, and the
protonation CHARMM-GUI assigns them is worth reading off step1_pdbreader.out
before the runs start, since a changed protonation state at one of those would
be a difference between arms that no substitution put there.
